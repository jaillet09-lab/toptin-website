If you are replacing an old roof in Queensland, the choice usually comes down to Colorbond steel or tile. Both can work. Here is an honest look at how they compare for a Brisbane home.

## Weight

This is the biggest practical difference. Concrete tiles are heavy. Colorbond steel is much lighter. On an older home, taking that weight off the frame is a real benefit, and it is one of the main reasons owners go from tile to Colorbond when the roof is due.

## Rain

Brisbane gets heavy, short downpours in storm season. A metal roof sheds water quickly and has fewer joins for water to find its way through. Tile roofs rely on overlaps, sarking and ridge bedding, and as those age, leaks become more likely.

## Heat

People often assume a metal roof is hotter. In practice, colour and insulation matter more than the material:

- **Colour.** Lighter colours reflect more of the sun's heat. Darker colours absorb more.
- **Insulation.** A blanket or sarking under the sheets makes a big difference to how hot the roof space gets.

A light Colorbond roof with a good blanket can perform well in a Queensland summer. A dark roof with nothing under it will be hotter, whatever it is made of.

## Noise

Rain on steel is louder than rain on tile. An insulation blanket under the sheets reduces this a lot, and most people find a well-insulated metal roof quiet enough. If noise matters to you, mention it at the quote.

## Look

Tile gives a traditional textured look that suits some homes. Colorbond comes in a wide range of colours and in corrugated or square-rib profiles. Corrugated suits character homes and Queenslanders, while square-rib profiles look cleaner and more modern. You can see the current range on the [Colorbond website](https://www.colorbond.com/).

## Maintenance

As concrete tiles age they need repointing, re-bedding and replacement of broken tiles. A Colorbond roof needs less day-to-day attention: keep gutters and valleys clear and check flashings after big storms.

## So which is better?

For most Brisbane homes we replace, owners choose Colorbond because it is lighter, handles heavy rain well and gives a clean, fresh look. Tile can still be the right choice for some homes, for example to match neighbouring roofs or a particular style.
