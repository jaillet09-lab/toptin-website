Plenty of Brisbane homes with old roofs also have solar panels. If the roof needs replacing, the panels have to come off first and go back on afterwards. It is a common job, and with a bit of planning it runs smoothly.

## Who does what

Disconnecting and reconnecting solar panels is electrical work. In Queensland it must be done by a licensed electrician. The panels themselves are handled as part of the roofing job.

A typical job looks like this:

1. **A licensed electrician disconnects the system** so it is safe to work on.
2. **We take the panels and rails off** and store them safely on site.
3. **We replace the roof.**
4. **We put the panels back** on the new roof.
5. **The electrician reconnects the system** and checks it is working.

## How the timing works

- **Mention the solar at the inspection.** The number of panels and how they are fixed affect the scope and the price.
- **Expect a few days without solar.** The system is off from when it is disconnected until it is reconnected.
- **Ask about rails and fixings.** Rails and brackets need to suit the new roof sheeting, and every fixing through the roof must be sealed.
- **Keep the paperwork.** Keep the electrician's paperwork with your house records.

## Checks before the job

Before the system is disconnected:

- Note how it is performing, for example from the inverter or monitoring app, so you can compare afterwards
- Check whether taking the panels off and putting them back affects any panel or inverter warranty
- Mention any batteries, isolators or cabling that run through the roof space

## Checks after the job

Once the system is reconnected:

- Check it is producing power again on the inverter or app
- Ask the electrician to confirm everything has been reconnected and tested
- Check that the fixings for rails and cables have been sealed
