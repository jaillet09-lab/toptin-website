Document-sized logos (transparent PNG).

Use these for invoices, PDFs, Word documents, slides and email signatures. The files in the main pack are 1600 to 2400 px wide, which is why they look far too big when dropped into a document.

  Primary   large 600 px wide, small 300 px wide   (headers, invoices, letterheads)
  Secondary large 400 px wide, small 200 px wide   (square or narrow spaces)
  Icon      large 256 px wide, small 128 px wide   (stamps, avatars, small marks)

Pick "for-white" files on white pages and "for-navy" files on Navy 700 (#1D2B4F) or Navy 900 (#0F162E).
Set the width in your tool, not the height, and keep the proportions locked.
Typical sizes on an A4 page: primary 45 to 60 mm wide, secondary 30 to 40 mm wide, icon 15 to 20 mm wide.
