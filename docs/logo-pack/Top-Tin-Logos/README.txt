TOP TIN ROOFING LOGOS (Logo 4, chosen 26 October 2026)

Folders
  1-Primary-horizontal   headers, documents, footers, signage, anywhere there is width
  2-Secondary-stacked    square or narrow spaces and centred layouts: social avatars, stamps, vehicle doors, form headers
  3-Icon                 the roof mark alone, where the name is already present or the space is tiny
  4-App-icons            app, home-screen, Apple touch and favicon files (icon on Navy 700)

Four versions of each logo, as SVG (vector, any size) and PNG (transparent background)
  Full-colour_for-white         navy, yellow and charcoal. Use on white or very light backgrounds.
  Reversed_for-navy             white and yellow. Use on Navy 700 (#1D2B4F) or Navy 900 (#0F162E).
  One-colour-navy_for-white     all navy. For one-colour printing, stamps and engraving on light material.
  One-colour-white_for-navy     all white. For one-colour printing and photos under a dark overlay.

Rules
  Grounds: white or Navy 700 / Navy 900 only. Do not recolour, stretch, outline, or add a box or shadow.
  Clear space: the height of the letter T on every side.
  Minimum size: primary 170 px wide on screen or 45 mm in print. Below that use the stacked logo, down to 110 px (30 mm), or the icon.
  Use the SVG for print, signage and anything that gets enlarged. Use the PNG for documents and slides.

Note
  TOP TIN is a vector trace of the original 1000 px artwork. For very large signage or vehicle graphics the printer may ask for an original outlined master file; ask Ethan for it.
