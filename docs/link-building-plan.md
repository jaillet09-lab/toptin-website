## Where to list (in order)

1. **Google Business Profile.** The most important. Service area business (hide the address), categories above,
   photos of real jobs, the 750-character description, and ask every happy customer for a review using the profile's
   review link (put it in `site.config.ts` → `reviews.googleReviewUrl`).
2. **Bing Places** (import from Google Business Profile).
3. **Apple Business Connect** (Apple Maps).
4. **Yellow Pages Australia**, **True Local**, **Hotfrog**, **Yelp Australia**, **Oneflare**, **hipages**, **ServiceSeeking**.
5. **Industry bodies** the business genuinely qualifies for, for example Master Builders Queensland or a roofing
   association. Only join and list if the membership is real.
6. **Supplier and manufacturer installer directories**, for example a Colorbond or roofing-supplier installer finder,
   only if Top Tin is eligible and listed.
7. **Local groups**: suburb community pages and local business directories, posting real finished jobs (with the
   homeowner's permission).

## Ongoing

- Add each finished job to the Projects page and Google Business Profile with suburb and photos.
- Ask for a Google review after every job.
- One new guide a month on a real customer question, linked to the matching service and suburb pages.
- Keep the NAP identical everywhere. If the phone number or email changes, update every listing.
